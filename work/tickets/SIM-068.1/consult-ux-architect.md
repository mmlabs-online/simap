# Consult — ux-architect — SIM-068.1

## Findings
- Slice 1 is merged. The page is a vertical stack inside a flex-col `<main>`. It runs header, `.aw-hero` (max 640 px), a row `max-w-[816px]` holding the 400 px `.aw-frame` card, then the footer. The card is centred and no world exists yet. `.aw-*` tokens in light and dark cover bg, ink, muted, line, strip, accent and serif-ink only. There are no room-floor, path or note tokens.
- Hero and card are stacked in one centre column, so a grid around "the centre column" must treat hero plus card as one cell. Because `<main>` is `justify-center`, the vertical centre of that cell is the page's visual midline. At 1440 px (and 1280 px) there is about 400 px of free width on each side of a 400 px card, so the 4 rooms do not fit a 2x2-flank-with-clearance layout without sizing.
- D52 allows pixel style on sign-in. Pixel font is banned only for body text, tables and forms. Room labels are art labels, so a mono or pixel label is allowed. There is no pixel font bundled and D45 forbids new fonts (the ticket also says "no new fonts"). So labels are best set in the existing sans with the `.aw-eyebrow` treatment (12 px, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.08em, bold).
- The cream/green palette (D85) is already established. Rooms painted from the office atlas have cool floors, which will clash with cream unless toned.
- The consult-1 advice to pick the scale by breakpoint stands. The new fact is that the ticket fixes rooms at 12x7 tiles. Confirm the tile size in `paintRoom` (likely 32 px, so a room is 384x224 at 1x). Two rooms of 384 plus a 400 card is 1168 px, which leaves no room for paths at 1280 px.

## Recommendations
1. **Composition (AC1, whole-number scale).** Use a 3-column grid `grid-cols-[1fr_minmax(0,400px)_1fr]` inside a container that is `max-w-[1360px]` and centred, in two rows. Rooms in the outer columns, 2 rows (top and bottom). Hero and card span both rows in column 2, centred. Rooms are fixed-size boxes at 1x (12x7 x tile). Do not shrink them with CSS. Put the rooms at the outer edges (`justify-self: start` on the left, `end` on the right) so spare width becomes path length, not room scale.
   - 1440 px: columns about 520 / 400 / 520. Each room is about 384 px wide with a 100+ px gutter to the card. This is the full mockup.
   - 1280 px: columns about 440 / 400 / 440 (after 20 px padding). Rooms 384 wide leave about 20-30 px to the card, so paths have almost no length. Options: (a) paint at 1x with a 10x6 crop, i.e. layouts 12x7 but displayed through a clipped box; (b) accept a 1280 px layout with paths shortened to short stubs and rooms pulled slightly under the card edge; (c) raise the cut-off to 1360. Recommended: keep the 1280 px cut as Ming chose, with rooms at 1x, and decide whether card-adjacent stubs plus vertical (top-to-bottom) paths are enough. Verify at exactly 1280 px.
   - Shift rooms vertically: top rooms align with the hero (top of the centre column), bottom rooms align with the card bottom. That uses the height instead of width, and the paths run mostly vertically between the top and bottom rooms on each side, then turn into the card's side.
2. **Paths.** CSS only. Each is a pair of absolutely positioned pieces inside the grid, using `border-style: dashed` or `repeating-linear-gradient` with a 4 px dash and 6 px gap, in `--aw-line` at lower contrast, 2 px thick, no animation. Route: bottom of the top room, vertical down to the top of the bottom room (left and right side), plus one horizontal stub from the vertical path into the card side at the card's mid-height. Keep right angles only (matches the pixel look, no SVG curves). Avatars (canvas or sprite at 1x) sit on the vertical segment, one per side, and one at the stub end. Avoid paths crossing the hero text: they must stay in the outer columns, not the centre.
3. **Notes (placement).** Two cards, one anchored to the lower inner edge of each top room (as in the mockup), `rotate(-2deg)` and `rotate(2deg)`, static, max width about 200 px, overlapping the room by about 20-30 px. At 1280 px the overlap can push into the path, so they sit on the outer side (toward the viewport edge), not the inner side, and must never overlap the card or hero. Style them like `.aw-strip`: `--aw-strip` background, `--aw-line` border, 1x pixel shadow `0 3px 0 var(--aw-line)`, text 14 px sans, ink colour (4.5:1 on strip). Italic serif only for the last words if Ming wants the mockup feel, else plain.
4. **Room labels vs D52.** Small chip on the room's top-left, 12 px uppercase sans with 0.08em tracking, on `--aw-strip` with a 1 px `--aw-line` border, `aria-hidden`. No pixel font. Text is decorative, but keep 4.5:1 anyway (the chip is real-contrast text, visible to sighted users).
5. **Visual weight (card dominates).** Rooms must be quieter than the card: paint in the canvas, then apply an overlay of the page bg at 15-25% (a `::after` with `background: var(--aw-bg); opacity:.2`), or `opacity: .85` on the room wrapper. Never use `filter` on canvas for scaling. Give rooms a 1 px `--aw-line` frame and no drop shadow; only the card keeps `box-shadow: 0 8px 0`. Room labels and notes use muted, not ink. Avatars are the only saturated spot. Idle avatars are static (no bob), and avatars on paths are no larger than the card avatar.
6. **Dark mode.** Add tokens: `--aw-room-tint` (the overlay colour = `--aw-bg`, opacity higher in dark, about 35-40%, to calm the bright atlas floors), `--aw-path` (light: `#c9bfa3`, dark: `#4a4d3f`), `--aw-note-bg/ink` (reuse strip/ink). Verify dark: the atlas floors are the same art in both modes, so the tint is what makes it fit the dark world. Placeholders use `--aw-strip` with a `--aw-line` border so they match in both themes. Check path contrast is low (decorative, no 3:1 requirement) but visible.
7. **1024-1279 px.** Show nothing of the world (ticket AC1: not rendered and no atlas fetch). Do not render a cut-down two-room variant (extra scope, atlas fetch, second layout). Keep the slice-1 stacked layout. Use the extra width by letting the hero keep its 640 px max. If it looks empty, a CSS-only static placeholder is not recommended. Gate with `matchMedia("(min-width: 1280px)")` in an effect and state default false, so SSR and first paint match (no hydration mismatch), and subscribe to change so resize across 1280 px mounts/unmounts correctly. A pure-CSS `hidden xl:grid` would still fetch the atlas if mounted, so the JS gate is required.
8. **DOM and tests.** Render `SmallWorld` after the card (or as an absolutely placed layer) inside `<main>` so tab order is unchanged. `AuthLayout` needs a relative wrapper, and `SignInScene` stays only for non-landing screens (Q4 default). Tests: render with `matchMedia` mocked both ways, asserts for `aria-hidden` on the wrapper, 4 labels, 2 notes, and still one `h1`.

## Risks
- 1280 px is the tight case: rooms of 384 px plus a 400 px card leave about 20-30 px gutters, so paths and notes have almost no space. Needs checking at exactly 1280, and also at about 1300-1360 px where rooms are not yet at the container edge.
- The atlas floors are cool grey and teal against the cream page. Tint overlays are a workaround, and could look muddy. Ming should review on a deployed build.
- `useAtlas` plus `matchMedia` gating can cause a flash or layout shift on first paint at wide screens: reserve the room boxes with fixed width/height placeholders to avoid CLS.
- Notes overlapping a room by a rotated amount can cause horizontal overflow at 1280 px; `overflow-x-clip` is already on the stage, but verify no clipped text.
- Vertical height: the hero plus card plus two room rows may exceed a 720-800 px viewport height, so the bottom rooms could fall below the fold at 1280x720 (acceptable, since they are decoration).
- Café with no counter or coffee machine may read as a lounge; label plus props (tables, plants) must carry it.
